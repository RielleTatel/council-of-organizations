## `/about` — About COA-Z

### Who We Are

The Council of the Organizations of the Ateneo - Zamboanga (COA-Z) is the sole alliance of all Ateneo de Zamboanga University College-accredited organizations. We serve as the primary bridge between El Consejo Atenista and the campus organizations it oversees — uniting them under a shared commitment to leadership, collaboration, and community.

For years, COA-Z has been the central body that creates platforms and programs for meaningful collaboration among member organizations and with external institutions alike. We amplify proactive leadership, support student organizations' initiatives, and build spaces where Ateneans can work together toward a greater good.

---

### Our Purpose

COA-Z fulfills six core functions as the governing alliance of AdZU's accredited organizations:

1. **As a representative body** — COA-Z defends and advances the general welfare of its member organizations, ensuring their voices are heard in university-wide decision-making.

2. **As an administrative body** — COA-Z advances member organizations' welfare and advocacies, and upholds its rights within university committees such as the Central Assembly and El Consejo Atenista.

3. **As an administrative body** — COA-Z streamlines processes and archives relevant data to preserve efficiency in transitions and operations, both for the Council as a whole and for individual member organizations.

4. **As a formative body** — COA-Z fosters the enrichment of core competencies, advocacies, and organizational development of its member organizations toward the holistic formation of their members and constituents.

5. **As a unitive body** — COA-Z provides platforms and fosters purposeful collaboration among member organizations and institutions, within and beyond Ateneo.

6. **Through service** — COA-Z fulfills its purpose through essential, effective, and adequate support services and initiatives, and through its role as collective representative and liaison to various external entities.

---

### Our Vision

The Council of Organizations of Ateneo - Zamboanga envisions itself as an empowered and collaborative community of competent organizations and holistically-formed students — ready to proactively respond to the challenges of their time and initiate positive changes within the Ateneo community and in greater society, through the Ignatian tradition of service and excellence.

---

### Our Mission

As the Council of Organizations of Ateneo - Zamboanga, we develop organizations as formative spaces — providing Ateneans venues for critical socio-political discourse, integrated business engagement, actualized spiritual growth, environmental action, physical and mental health, cultural rootedness and exploration, creative communication, artistic expression, social immersion to uphold human dignity, and a greater appreciation and innovation through science and technology.

We empower organizations to be united, effective, collaborative, and sustainable through proper guidance, dialogue, and support.

---

### Core Principles

**Representative Democracy**
All Ateneo student organizations are present and united in creating a collaborative community that develops student leaders to become empowered, active, competent, and holistically formed.

**Equality and Accessibility**
All Ateneo student organizations and their constituents receive fair and equal treatment, and benefit from and with each other in order to form a progressive and effective coalition.

**Transparency and Accountability**
All Ateneo student organizations are held fully responsible for decisions that impact the community, with no exemptions.

## `/leadership` — Leadership

### Executive Board

The Executive Board of COA-Z comprises five offices, each addressing distinct organizational concerns of the Council.

---

#### Office of the Chairperson

The Office of the Chairperson leads the Council, represents COA-Z to the university and external bodies, and sets the direction of the Council's programs and advocacies.

**Positions:**
- Chairperson, Council of the Organizations of Ateneo - Zamboanga
- Executive Secretary to the Chairperson
- Undersecretary for Internal Affairs
- Undersecretary for External Affairs
- Chief Legal and Policy Adviser

---

#### Office of the Secretary-General

The Office of the Secretary-General oversees internal records, institutional memory, and the transparency functions that keep the Council accountable to its member organizations.

**Positions:**
- Secretary-General, Council of the Organizations of Ateneo - Zamboanga
- Undersecretary for Transparency

---

#### Office of Communications

The Office of Communications manages how COA-Z reaches and engages its constituents — from social media to content strategy.

**Positions:**
- Communications Head
- Social Media Handler
- Content Manager

---

#### Office of Creatives and Branding

The Office of Creatives and Branding shapes the visual identity of COA-Z and documents the Council's events and milestones.

**Positions:**
- Creatives Head
- Documentation Head
- Creatives Associate
- Documentation Associate

---

#### Office of Finance

The Office of Finance ensures the fiscal integrity of the Council, managing funds and supporting the financial needs of member organizations.

**Positions:**
- Finance Head
- Treasurer
- Subsidy Officer

---

### Buklod Atenista Envoy Committee

The Buklod Atenista Envoy Committee represents COA-Z in the national Buklod Atenista network, composed of the Chairperson and other key officers as appointed by the Chairperson.

### Member Organizations

COA-Z member organizations are grouped into six clusters based on their organizational vision and focus. Each cluster fosters a distinct sphere of student formation.

---

#### Culture, Arts, and Multimedia Cluster

Organizations centered on arts (theater, music, dance), multimedia (technology-driven creative expression), and culture (preservation and promotion of heritage and subcultures).

#### Socio-Civic and Political Cluster

Organizations dedicated to fostering social responsibility, civic engagement, and political awareness through community service projects, advocacy campaigns, and educational events.

Academic organizations from fields including Accountancy, Business and Management, STEM, and the Humanities and Social Sciences. Fosters intellectual development, critical thinking, and career formation.

Organizations promoting holistic well-being, healthy lifestyles, sports development, and environmental stewardship — advancing physical health, sportsmanship, emergency response, and care for the environment.
